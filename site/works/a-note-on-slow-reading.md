# Why a small screen helps

A page of paper shows you a few hundred words and nothing else. A small reader does the same. *Nothing else* is the point: there is no feed to check and no tab to open.

# What to try

- Read one chapter, then stop.
- Come back the next day to the **same** page.
- Notice what you remembered.

The habit is older than any device. The device only makes it easier to keep.
