# The Visitor

Nobody came to the house on the hill, so when the gate creaked, Ines put down her book and listened. The visitor was a boy with a *very* large dog.

"Is this the place with the clock?" he asked.

She said it was. She did not say that the clock had been wrong for six years.

# The Clock

The boy looked at the clock for a long time. **It was ten past four.** It had always been ten past four.

- He did not fix it.
- She did not ask him to.

By the time the dog fell asleep on the step, it was nearly dark, and neither of them could say where the afternoon had gone.
